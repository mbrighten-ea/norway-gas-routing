# Minimum capacity database specification

This is a design, not an implemented database/downloader. [Coverage matrix](network_forecast_capacity_coverage.csv), [public source register](network_forecast_public_sources.csv) and the earlier [retrieval audit](public_capacity_findings.md) define its feasible scope. Use public operator evidence for technical ratings; keep private production forecasts in a separate source contract.

| Entity | Minimum fields |
|---|---|
| Asset/connection | Stable asset ID; physical endpoints; operator; plant/pipe/segment/point/aggregate kind; rich/dry/entry/exit/accounting boundary; operator/point/direction/EIC keys; shared/dependency group; active dates; model mapping and quality status. Preserve foundation versus executable IDs. |
| Dated rating | Rating ID; asset; value and original unit; reference temperature, absolute pressure, water basis; gas/energy/GCV definition; nameplate, operational technical or commercial category; valid-from/to; date precision/uncertain change window; source/vintage; verification status. |
| Restriction event | Event ID/version; affected boundary and dependency group; planned/unplanned; timezone-aware start/end; residual absolute capacity or reduction with units; status/cancellation; overlap semantics; first-published and revision-published timestamps; original notice; uncertainty. |
| Daily effective capacity | Asset and gas-day start/end; nameplate, operational and commercial quantities **separately**; covered duration; unknown flag; rating/event lineage; conversion/GCV inputs; source vintage and calculation version. Distinguish source observations from derived values. |
| Source/publication vintage | URL/request/export parameters; original bytes/hash; retrieval time; original publication time or unknown; last revision time; source effective interval; supersession; archive coverage/access limitations; confidence. |
| Monthly optimiser view | Asset, month, month-boundary convention, summed quantity/unit, covered/unknown duration, contributing daily/rating/event IDs, as-of cutoff, reconstruction/forecast mode, calculation version and readiness. This is a reproducible derived view, not a competing input authority. |

Effective time and knowledge time are separate. A current page does not receive an invented 2021 valid-from; an autumn-2023 upgrade remains an uncertain window until a dated operating limit is verified. Store revisions rather than replacing history. A historical API `lastUpdateDateTime` can be a later revision, not proof of original publication. Genuine forecast-origin views include only publications known at that origin; revised reconstructions are labelled separately.

For a daily **quantity** in million reference cubic metres, monthly bcm is `sum(Q_d)/1000`. For a volume **rate**, integrate its verified effective rate over elapsed subintervals, respecting the source's reference-day duration. Split at rating/outage boundaries and month edges; prorate partial outages only when the notice defines a compatible rate or residual quantity. Do not multiply a kWh/day gas-day quantity by 24 again. The retained ENTSOG short/long days show duration effects; they are not automatically outages.

Keep source gas days, normally 06:00 CET/CEST, timezone-aware through DST, leap days and revisions. Specify whether the optimiser month uses gas-day labels or calendar-time integration. Use half-open intervals and retain original endpoint semantics. ENTSOG queries may return validity intervals extending outside the requested window: intersect intervals with the requested days and validate pagination, duplicates, nulls, NA flags and conflicting overlaps. Row count is not covered-day count.

For supported energy-to-volume conversion, `kWh / (kWh/reference-m³) / 1e9` gives bcm. Require a source-supported capacity-basis GCV and matching gross/net, temperature, pressure and water conventions. Do not invent a calorific value, reuse a dashboard conversion constant or apply an observed-flow GCV to an engineering rating without evidence. Sm³ and Nm³ have different reference temperatures; do not equate them silently. A 10 bcm/year rating cannot automatically define each day's technical limit or a leap-year convention.

Deduplicate revisions/cancellations of the same event. Identical/shared restrictions count once; absolute residual caps on the same boundary use the binding residual. Sum reductions only when operator definitions establish independence. Otherwise mark overlap unresolved. Do not subtract events from an operational technical series already reflecting them. A plant event and upstream field events may represent the same lost throughput, and aggregated UMM impacts can reflect network flexibility rather than a pipe's engineering limit. Missing notices leave availability unknown, not full capacity and not zero.

Forward maintenance uses published future notices available at the forecast origin and explicitly recorded assumptions for uncovered time. Historical realised restrictions must not be copied into future years as facts. Future nameplate changes require development/commissioning evidence with effective-date precision. Retain the current optimiser's assumptions until a separate approved implementation resolves Kollsnes, Kårstø rich/net boundaries, point/pipe identities, Danish/Polish conversion conventions and German/Dutch receiving clusters.
