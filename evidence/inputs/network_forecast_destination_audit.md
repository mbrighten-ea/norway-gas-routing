# Destination observation definitions

The reference is the unchanged [2025 flexibility diagnostic](../../archive/20261009-14_routing-flexibility/report.md), using official Snøhvit net production and revised inputs. It is provisional reconstruction evidence, not a genuine forecast. Every individual country envelope retains maximum total exports; different countries' extrema cannot be achieved together or summed as redirection potential.

The seven-country targets are retained EA monthly **allocation/QA proxies**, selected by explicit `*_flow_qa` registry keys. The foundation configuration separately derives NL from Emden EPT1 and DE from combined Emden/Dornum minus NL. Those foundation expressions are not the POC's independently validated country-delivery dataset. UK/France/Belgium physical receiving points similarly do not establish commercial final destinations.

Read-only dashboard source declarations corroborate important definition hazards: `norway_cache.py:114` uses a fixed **0.095 bcm/TWh** conversion for EPT1-derived estimates; its current-month filling uses daily terminal feeds while historical monthly legs are separate sources. That constant is an existing dashboard implementation, **not an approved model conversion or supported capacity-basis calorific value**. The workbook's `Gassco_raw!B1/B2` references StFergus, St_Fergus and Fields_Delivering_into_SEGAL together; prior retained checks identify a handover/overlap risk. A source-expression reference proves neither current coverage nor safe summation. Dashboard code was not executed and current-period outcome caches were not inspected.

The public [Gassco map](https://gassco.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Det-norske-gasstransportsystemet-2024.pdf), [ENTSOG definitions/API](https://transparency.entsog.eu/api/archiveDirectories/8/api-manual/TP_REG715_Documentation_TP_API%20-%20v2.1.pdf) and [public capacity findings](public_capacity_findings.md) support physical/point distinctions; they do not prove the country proxies' transformations. Receiving-point histories have working public retrieval, but no source-aligned monthly physical volume reconciliation is completed here. Possible conversion, routing, scope and allocation errors remain **hypotheses** until that reconciliation. No price causality, capacity fit or source correction was inferred from 2025 errors.
