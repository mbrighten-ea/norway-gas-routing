# Network, capacity and forecast-source audit

**Two proposed field reallocations meet the route-and-forecast test for a frozen workbook-based production scenario: Halten Øst to Kårstø and Urd to Kårstø.** Brage, Tune and Balder remain conditional on entry/accounting or dated export-mode evidence. Trym has a forecast but cannot defensibly enter the existing Norwegian EPII/North Sea Entry branch; it needs a separate Danish source boundary. These are audit classifications for a separately approved implementation. No physical correction, source activation or price objective was implemented.

All six priority fields have **51 finite individual cached forecast months, October 2026–December 2030**. No individual EA forecast SID was found for them; the verified source is the retained production workbook. Sixteen selected field forecasts match live EA exports exactly across all 51 months. National production reconciles exactly to workbook non-LNG production plus Snøhvit; non-flexible production equals national total minus Troll minus Oseberg. Thus the six fields already belong to the total/aggregate, and a correction must **reallocate residual**, not add supply. This establishes a usable frozen snapshot, not a maintained automated feed or historical forecast-origin vintage.

Seven verified historical Directorate SIDs were appended, all optional inactive references. The six field SIDs reproduce the 1.815139 bcm known 2025 residual contributions, including Halten Øst 1.167452 bcm. The seventh is the official monthly national total; its revised 2025 sum is 121.839 bcm versus retained EA 121.7718 bcm. Neither difference nor the new actual series changes the optimiser's production accounting. [SID report](network_forecast_sid_audit.md) lists every exact addition and verification status.

## Historical capacities and future use

The [capacity matrix](network_forecast_capacity_coverage.csv) covers 19 executable bottlenecks, 15 retained foundation entries and eight unconstrained proxy/transfer arcs. [Exact extracted model inventory](network_forecast_model_inventory.json), [production components](network_forecast_production_inventory.csv) and [connections](network_forecast_connections.csv) retain IDs, units and assumptions. Foundation null capacities are not active POC bottlenecks.

Eight additional publicly rated corridors are recorded as **unmodelled**, without invented model IDs: Åsgard Transport, Norne Gas Transport, rich-gas Statpipe, the internal Sleipner–Draupner Zeepipe segment, Tampen Link, Gjøa Gas Pipe, Utsira High Gas Pipeline and Polarled. Their 2024 ratings support a future topology audit, not immediate limits on the model's net-dry aggregate feeds. Collapsed CORE omits the separately rated internal transfer, and the direct-UK proxy omits distinct feeder limits. Resolve routing direction, shared use and rich/dry conversion before separate implementation; a global CORE or Nyhamna limit inferred from one segment would be wrong.

The existing public audit verified twelve ENTSOG operator/point/direction histories. North Sea Entry and both Faxe directions have complete latest-revised technical interval coverage for 2023–2025 in energy units; 2022 starts at October commissioning. Receiving-point histories exist for Zeebrugge, Dunkerque, UK and German/Dutch landings. Two NPT point combinations are all zero and do not provide useful Norpipe engineering ratings. Public Gassco 2024 pipeline specifications are usable snapshots; a complete 2021–2025 map/plant/pipe engineering-availability archive is not demonstrated. Historical UMM filters/export yielded empty/template responses; GMP was inaccessible. UMM publication thresholds and aggregate impacts would leave additional gaps even if an archive were retrieved.

A new bounded North Sea Entry request reconfirmed **321.864 GWh/source gas day** in September 2025. It returned one long validity interval, not three daily rows; the requested days must be intersected with it. No bcm conversion was made without capacity-basis GCV. [Public source register](network_forecast_public_sources.csv) retains URLs, publication/retrieval methods and access limitations. Daily latest-revised **point** histories can be reconstructed for 2023–2025; reliable daily operational histories for **every upstream model asset** cannot. Forward maintenance and rating assumptions through 2030 still need explicit source/vintage treatment. The [database specification](network_forecast_capacity_database.md) describes this without implementing ingestion.

## Price-response decision

The network is credible enough for a **bounded diagnostic price sensitivity within its declared provisional constraints**, once the separate price-data feasibility requirements are met. It is not sufficient for a validated production price-response forecast or a causal claim about observed allocation. Kollsnes disposal is unreachable by price redirection, DE/NL freedom omits real receiving/commercial restrictions, and several observation targets lie outside physical envelopes or use signed accounting definitions. These can distort apparent price benefits. Resolve source/observation definitions and preserve identical inputs when comparing objectives; do not calibrate physical parameters against 2025 errors.

The 2025 official-Snøhvit scenario remains a provisional revised-input reconstruction. Training ends December 2024; 2025 remains validation; January–September 2026 remains locked and unscored. All forward numeric checks start October 2026 and use confirmed forecasts. No routing preference, capacity, maintenance, objective, source aggregate, ordinary Snøhvit guard or historical result was changed.
