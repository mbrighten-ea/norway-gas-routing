# Public historical capacity audit

Audited 9 October 2026. Target: January 2021–December 2025; earlier retrieval tested selectively. **A reliable public database for every modelled physical constraint is not yet supported.** A useful partial database is feasible: dated specifications, a Kollsnes upgrade window, and complete latest-revised 2023–2025 technical entry/exit capacity intervals for Danish/Polish transit. Complete upstream daily engineering availability and contemporaneous publication vintages remain missing. This is evidence for a Stage 2 decision, not authority to change assumptions.

Evidence: [asset matrix](public_capacity_coverage.csv), [source register](public_capacity_sources.csv), [actual point coverage](public_capacity_point_coverage.csv), [historical samples](public_capacity_samples.csv), [request/hash manifest](public_capacity_audit_manifest.json). Original representative downloads are in [data/raw/public_capacity_audit](../../data/raw/public_capacity_audit/). Disposable probes remain in `.scratch/runs/20261009-15_public-capacity-audit/`.

## Model inventory and physical boundaries

`config/model.yaml:poc.assets` contains **19 executable capacity assumptions**. The matrix extracts their exact IDs, values and source keys. Three plants are KOLLSNES_EXPORT 143, NYHAMNA_EXPORT 84 and KARSTO_PROCESSING 95 mcm/day. Fourteen pipeline ratings are OGT 35, HEIMDAL_STATPIPE 35, KARSTO_STATPIPE 12, DRAUPNER_EKOFISK_STATPIPE 32.4, LANGELED_NORTH 74.7, LANGELED_SOUTH 73.8, ZEEPIPE_IIA 77, ZEEPIPE_IIB 80, EUROPIPE_I 48, EUROPIPE_II 73.5, NORPIPE 34, VESTERLED 35, ZEEPIPE_EXPORT 42.2 and FRANPIPE 54.2 mcm/day. Transit assumptions are NORTH_SEA_ENTRY 27.4 mcm/day and BALTIC_PIPE 10 bcm/year. The reader converts the latter to `10*1000/365` mcm/day, including leap years; this existing convention is not a verified daily operator rating.

The retained foundation `assets` block has **15 separate rows**, all with null technical capacities, including NYBRO_EXIT, EMDEN_RECEIVING and UK_DIRECT_ROUTE; inactive EMDEN_RECEIVING and DE_DIRECT_ROUTE are identified. Foundation aliases are not additional active POC bottlenecks.

Shared EUROPIPE_II constrains the corridor before Germany/Denmark splitting. NORTH_SEA_ENTRY is shared before DK_OFFTAKE and Baltic transit; BALTIC_PIPE constrains the PL route. Constraints must apply to summed users, not separately grant the entire capacity to each destination. CORE collapses Sleipner/Draupner transfers and has no aggregate capacity; the map's separate Sleipner–Draupner S 55 MSm3/day segment is not independently constrained. CORE, EUROPIPE_II_SPLIT, EMDEN_POOL, DANISH_TRANSIT and FAXE have no separate engineered ratings. EUROPIPE_II_DORNUM, DK_OFFTAKE, PL_DELIVERY, SLEIPNER_CORE, EKOFISK_INJECTION, NL_ALLOCATION_PROXY and DE_ALLOCATION_PROXY have no independent asset capacity. Generated disposal arcs are accounting routes. None of these omissions is filled by this audit.

## Usable specifications and conflicts

**Pipelines:** the downloaded [Gassco 2024 map](https://gassco.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Det-norske-gasstransportsystemet-2024.pdf) corroborates all fourteen active pipeline numbers and physical endpoints. It is a nameplate/transport snapshot, not a daily schedule. Internal February 2024 naming and December 2024 upload path do not establish an effective start date. Targeted searches did not verify a complete Gassco 2021–2025 map archive. No capacity was inferred from flows.

**Kollsnes:** [Norwegian Petroleum](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/produksjon-og-eksport/landanlegg/) distinguishes 146.5 million Sm3/day rich processing from 143 dry export. The internally dated 2019 Gassco brochure explicitly gives export capacity 144.5 MSm3/day. Current Gassco pages conflict: Norwegian narrative 143/table 156; English narrative 143/table 144.5. [Gassco's 2023 report](https://gassco.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Gassco-aarsrapport-2023-ENGELSK.pdf) documents a successful upgrade in autumn 2023 (+8%); [Equinor's 6 January 2025 announcement](https://www.equinor.com/news/20250106-troll-highest-natural-gas-production-ever) states 144.5→156 million Sm3/day. The 2020 MEG announcement's planned 2022/2023 installation is not an effective date. The numerical change is credible; the exact operating day, 143 versus 144.5 baseline, and upgraded net-dry boundary need resolution. Do not set 156 from 1 January 2023 or convert the report's 46 TWh/year potential using an invented calorific value.

**Kårstø:** approximately 95 million Sm3/day is rich-gas inlet processing. Equinor's 2024 report labels 97 as gas-processing capacity. Neither establishes 95 or 97 net dry export or a dated expansion. Liquids removal, gas composition and operating conditions prevent direct substitution into the model's dry-gas balance. **Nyhamna:** Gassco explicitly reports export capacity 84 million Sm3/day, corroborated by the annual report; a dated unchanged 2021–2025 history remains unverified. Offshore Directorate facility pages establish facility identity/start/status, not a historical rating database; the Kollsnes NPDID 286729 page was retrieved.

## Historical retrieval demonstrated

**ENTSOG:** the public API and CSV export work. Original `operationaldatas` JSON was downloaded for 12 operator/point/direction combinations and checked for temporal interval coverage, null/NA records and conflicting overlaps. The companion CSV contains 60 point-year coverage rows. All twelve cover every 2023–2025 gas day syntactically; the two NPT combinations contain zero throughout and are **not useful Norpipe engineering ratings**. North Sea Entry and both Faxe directions cover 92 gas days in 2022, starting 1 October; their 2021 absence is precommissioning, not a missing-data imputation. Other tested points cover 2021–2025. Three selective 2019–2020 queries returned 404; earlier API coverage was not demonstrated by those probes. This is not a claim that all earlier data are unavailable. The ENTSOG map catalog lists earlier editions; the actual 2021 XLSX was retrieved and inspected.

Useful transit identities are Energinet DK-TSO-0001 / ITP-00630 / entry (North Sea Entry), DK-TSO-0001 / ITP-10009 / exit (Faxe), and GAZ-SYSTEM PL-TSO-0002 / ITP-10009 / entry (Faxe). Their normal daily technical quantity is 321,864,000 kWh/d = **321.864 GWh per source gas day**. Energy-to-bcm conversion is deliberately blank. ENTSOG short/long gas days show 308.453/335.275 GWh, consistent with 23/25 hours at 13.411 GWh/hour; these are not outages. A bounded CSV request with `periodize=0` was retained. `periodize=1` expanded full matched validity intervals outside the requested period; always clip and check pagination rather than trusting requested dates or counting rows as days.

The Zeebrugge response reported count 1402/total 1403. A follow-up at offset 1402 returned an already-included 31 December 2025 record with identical capacity and validity; the discrepancy is retained in provenance. Temporal coverage remains complete, but a production retriever must validate both pagination and duplicate identities. [Verification results](public_capacity_verification.json) record inventory, coverage and link checks.

Dated reductions are retrievable, but causes and publication origin are not proven. Examples: North Sea Entry 1 August 2024 reports 168 GWh/day; Faxe's Danish and Polish sides differ during commissioning and on 2 and 9 October 2023. They cannot be averaged or silently treated as interchangeable. The [Danish report](https://en.energinet.dk/media/xlmiu5pq/report-on-security-of-gas-supply-2023.pdf) and [Polish commissioning announcement](https://www.gaz-system.pl/en/for-media/press-releases/2022/november/31-11-2022-baltic-pipe-has-reached-its-maximum-capacity.html) establish limited operation from 1 October 2022 and full Baltic Pipe operation on 30 November. The announcement's URL typo is not its displayed publication date.

Receiving technical histories also exist for Zeebrugge, Dunkerque, Easington, St Fergus, Emden EPT1 and Dornum. They describe transmission entry points/allocations or aggregate terminals, **not** proven ratings for Zeepipe, Franpipe, Langeled, Vesterled or Europipe. St Fergus includes other routes; Nybro entry is distinct from North Sea Entry; German/Dutch points may share clustered capacity. The 2021 ENTSOG workbook explicitly limits a GTS Emden EPT/Bunde cluster. Do not sum overlapping TSO allocations or assign a terminal change to an upstream pipe without operator evidence.

`Firm Available` samples were also retrieved: North Sea Entry on 1 September 2023 reports 2.874782 GWh/day while `Firm Technical` reports 321.864. Commercial availability is unsold capacity, not engineering headroom. `lastUpdateDateTime` is a revision timestamp; some retrieved historical ratings carry 2026 revisions. This audit supports latest-revised reconstruction, not verified information available at a historical forecast origin.

**Outages:** Gassco historical filters for ten plants/terminals, plus Kollsnes INACTIVE and DISMISSED queries, returned empty event tables. The HTTP-200 XLSX export contained headers and a dummy 2016 row (`xxx/yyy/sss`), not actual historical events. GMP timed out, including an approved retry. No public historical outage archive was demonstrated. The [UMM principles](https://umm.gassco.no/resources/Principles%20for%20publication%20on%20umm.pdf) publish selected impacts above 5 MSm3/day, depend on network flexibility, and can net overlapping field/plant work; even a complete UMM archive would not necessarily enumerate every physical restriction. Missing events mean unknown availability, not full capacity. GAZ-SYSTEM public pages were readable via the web tool but direct requests returned a JavaScript challenge; SWI returned a shell without a verified historical export.

## Minimum database design and monthly limits

Keep five linked tables, retaining original observations rather than overwriting revisions:

| Table | Minimum fields |
|---|---|
| Assets | asset_id, physical identity/endpoints, operator, point keys/direction, plant/pipe/point/aggregate type, model boundary, sharing/dependency group, active dates, mapping status |
| Ratings | rating_id, asset_id, value/unit, temperature/absolute pressure/water basis, rich/dry and energy/GCV basis, technical/nameplate/operational/commercial category, valid_from/to, effective-date precision, verification status, source_id, vintage_id |
| Events | event_id, revision, asset/boundary, planned/unplanned, start/end/timezone, absolute residual capacity or reduction/unit, dependency/overlap group, status/cancellation, uncertainty, first_published_at, revision_published_at, source/vintage |
| Daily capacities | asset, gas-day start/end, rating/event/vintage lineage, nameplate quantity, operational quantity, commercial quantity separately, covered duration, unknown flag, conversion inputs, derivation version |
| Sources/vintages | URL, request/export parameters, retrieval time, original publication time or unknown, archive availability, bytes/hash, original interval, revision/supersession lineage, evidence quality |

Effective time and publication time must be separate. An autumn-only change retains an uncertain window; a current page does not receive a fabricated 2021 valid-from. Maintain reconstruction and origin-available views with explicit missing vintage flags.

For a verified daily volume quantity `Q_d` in million reference m3, monthly bcm is `sum(Q_d)/1000`, over explicitly defined gas days. For a partial outage, split time at rating/event boundaries and integrate verified residual rate over each segment; if a rating is a per-day reference rate, prorate by its documented reference duration. A published kWh/d gas-day quantity already includes that day's duration: do not multiply it by 24 again. Sum energy first; convert only with an operator-supported capacity-basis GCV in compatible kWh/reference-m3 and matching gross/net, pressure, temperature and water conditions. `kWh / (kWh/m3) / 1e9` gives bcm. Observed-flow calorific values and the Energinet forecast's fixed 12.1 kWh/Nm3 are not automatically capacity-basis conversions.

Preserve timezone-aware 06:00 CET/CEST source boundaries, DST and leap days. Specify whether the monthly limit uses gas-day labels or calendar-month elapsed time; split boundary gas days if the latter. Use half-open validity intervals. Revision/cancellation supersedes the same event. Combine identical/shared restrictions once; absolute residual limits on the same boundary use the binding cap. Add independent reductions only when operator definitions establish independence; otherwise leave overlap unresolved. Never subtract reductions from an ENTSOG technical series already reflecting them. Network/plant aggregated events require dependency mapping. Unknown days make the monthly operational limit incomplete, not zero and not full nameplate.

## Stage 2 decision and next steps

Proceed only with a bounded public point-history pilot in original energy units and a specification register. Before a complete volumetric engineering database, resolve: (1) Kollsnes baseline, dry boundary and exact autumn 2023 effective day; (2) Kårstø rich-to-net-dry definition and 95/97 discrepancy; (3) historical Gassco rating and outage retrieval, including publication vintages and sub-threshold gaps; (4) receiver-point versus upstream-pipe mappings and shared German/Dutch clusters; (5) Sm3/Nm3 conditions and supported capacity-basis GCV for Denmark/Poland; (6) Faxe side differences and commissioning restrictions; (7) event overlap semantics, gas-day monthly policy and unknown-day treatment. These require lead-modeller decisions/operator evidence before implementation. No full database/downloader, optimiser, assumptions, splits or historical results changed.

Pre-existing protected configuration/source/data/test/result files were hash-checked unchanged. `outputs/README.md` changed after the initial hash snapshot, although this audit made no writes to it; it was preserved and the unexplained before/after difference is recorded in the manifest. No staging or commits occurred. The manifest records requests and retained raw hashes. No private Booking, GREP, shipper or EA capacity evidence was used. One initial UMM HTML parser inadvertently returned an embedded live nominations block despite historical filters; it was excluded from curated evidence and subsequent parsing was restricted to event tables. No 2026 holdout outcomes were evaluated or scored. Capacity validity/revision metadata extending beyond 2025 was retained as original provenance; analysis was clipped to 2021–2025 gas days.
