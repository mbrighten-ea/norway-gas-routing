## Exact forecast origins

contain field/type labels, production start and exhaustion dates, decline-profile
lookups and occasional manual assumptions. October 2026 is **row 465**, December
2030 is **row 515**. These future cells have 51/51 finite saved monthly rate values
from October 2026 through December 2030; a saved zero is preserved as a zero.

The previous candidate-forecast CSV incorrectly labelled the six October 2026
addresses as `GK18`, `EQ18`, `ER18`, `CL18`, `CI18`, `EU18`. `D18` says **Current
month**, and `E18=EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1` has a saved October 2026 date. The field
cells in that header row are blank. This duplicated date caused the earlier
address-selection error. The correct addresses are the six **row 465** cells.
All 306 previous numeric values match by field/month; no historical result or
archived file was rewritten. The current-month formula is not a publication
timestamp or proof that underlying source inputs were refreshed.

reinterpreted as a different shutdown convention. Static precedent chains in the
CSV include alternatives that are inactive when the end-date branch returns zero.

## Historical sources, export identifiers and refresh

Historical formulas select a field from `NPD_raw!E5:EZ5`, match the month in
`NPD_raw!D6:D500`, and convert stored monthly bcm to mcm/day as
`monthly_bcm / calendar_days * 1000`. Resolved October/December 2025 seed addresses
and their literal monthly values are in the exact-cell CSV. The historical field
columns are BE (Halten Øst), DU (Trym), DV (Tune), W (Brage), S (Balder), DZ (Urd).

`NPD_raw!D1`, `N1` and `T1` contain Shooju wildcard/filter declarations for
Directorate marketed gas. They are discovery expressions, not exact individual
EA forecast SIDs. `NPD_raw!A1` identifies the Directorate national total. The exact
six individual Directorate SIDs in the summary come from the previously verified
registry/audit, **not** from a literal individual SID in these six header columns.

Formula structure changes from Directorate lookups to forecasts in **August 2026
(row 463)** for all six fields. This is the workbook handover. Earlier API metadata
checks reported an October 2026 handover for the exported EA aggregates; those
are distinct vintages/interfaces. No January–September 2026 numeric actuals or
destination outcomes were accessed here. June 2026 lookup formulas are retained
as structural references only, with their cached values withheld.

The worksheet's `IA2` label is **SJ Write**. `IE2` holds the already-registered
national EA SID and `ID2` the already-registered non-flexible aggregate SID.
`ID465=IE465-SUM(IB465:IC465)`. The small-field total `HE465=SUM(CD465:FY465)`
includes five candidates; `HF465=SUM(GI465:GU465)` includes Halten Øst. These enter
the calculated national and non-flexible outputs. The named individual EA export
columns beside them represent other fields; none identifies these six candidates.
The existing aggregate forecasts already include their volumes: sourcing leaves
would require reallocation, not additional national supply.

Operationally, maintaining these forecasts would require refreshing/importing
Directorate history with the established source queries, maintaining the profile
literals, start/end dates and field overrides, recalculating in the provider's
approved workbook environment, then publishing a dated workbook/export. **The
exact refresh add-in, unattended job, write-back procedure and responsible owner
are not verified by the saved file.** No connections, query tables, external-link
parts or VBA project are retained. The seven defined names are hidden Solver
settings for the Troll regression and supply no forecast SID. Custom sheet binary
properties are GUIDs; the custom XML contains an empty Argus query-store element,
not a production-source definition. Refreshing the historical table alone does
not maintain Halten's manual scale/addition or Balder's literal schedule.

The original source trail therefore ends at stored profile/manual inputs and
saved Directorate raw cells with source-query declarations. No missing SID is
invented. Automated use through 2030 remains conditional on an approved maintained
workbook/provider export and publication vintages. This refines source lineage;
it does not change the earlier frozen values, approve field mappings, or promote
the provisional reconstruction into a validated forecast.
