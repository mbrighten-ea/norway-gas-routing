Why 2025 destination allocation underperformed EA

December 2024 origin; twelve 2025 deliveries. This uses immutable F2.10 forecasts and evaluation data, not fresh source queries or a new backtest. It remains a revision-tolerant reconstruction. The forward model and all F2.10 files are unchanged. The common basket is boundary-adjusted UK plus France/Belgium plus Germany/Netherlands; it excludes non-Gassco UK packets and DK/PL, and is not the full Norwegian export total.

Conclusion: the network modestly improves on static 2023 shares overall, but EA allocates the common volume substantially better. The largest errors reflect static historical preferences and weak identification of flexible UK/France allocation, alongside continental corridor constraints. UK-direct supply and Kollsnes alone do not explain them.

What the constraints actually identify

Europipe I (48), Europipe II (73.5) and Norpipe (34) together bound DE/NL plus DK/PL at 155.5 mcm/day in this network. The saved DK/PL allocation consumes 0.752 bcm in May/December and 0.728 in June, leaving DE/NL at 4.068/3.937 bcm. All three corridors bind in these months. The DE/NL maximum over physically feasible maximum-export solutions is higher: 4.821/4.665 bcm, by reducing Nordic allocation. Thus the reported DE/NL ceiling mixes physical corridor limits with preference-selected DK/PL deliveries. Individual DE/NL splits are proxies.

France/Belgium jointly has a 96.4 mcm/day envelope (Franpipe 54.2 + Zeepipe 42.2). At fixed forecast supply, minimum UK follows total exports minus these five continental capacities, corrected for non-Gassco UK production. Those cuts are mathematically necessary in the stated model; their capacity vintages and common gas-volume conditions remain provisional. No retained evidence here proves a wrong topology or a real operating bottleneck in 2025.

Recommendation to Core

Investigate first the assumption that the annual 2023 preference rule meaningfully identifies the flexible UK-versus-France allocation. It misses the observed seasonal share pattern and permits materially different, equally optimal final allocations. Establish what pre-origin physical or historical evidence should determine that allocation and how unresolved ties should be disclosed. Do not fit a preferred point to 2025, change weights or pick a Kollsnes rating from this sample.

Priorities by evidence: (1) static monthly preferences and unresolved final-objective ties; (2) the combined continental envelope and preference-selected Nordic diversion; (3) revision-tolerant production/source mix and missing maintenance information. The last two remain provisional inputs, not demonstrated specification errors. This audit cannot attribute realised outages or demand changes without new evidence, and does not initiate such an investigation.

Validation: F2.10 hashes and all frozen accounting checks pass; 54 bound queries are optimal and satisfy node/capacity checks. Independent share/error identities, monthly ordering, baseline derivation and selected-month ranking are checked. Focused regression results are retained in provenance. No code, configuration, network, objective, weights or protected 2026 actuals changed.
