Supply and modelled bottlenecks

Unclassified supply

UNMAPPED supply has no export route in the saved model. It remains separate from gas attributed to a mapped production source.

Mapped-source disposal

Mapped gas can be disposed by the model when its infrastructure limits prevent export. This is not proof of actual Norwegian production curtailment.

A binding capacity row does not alone establish the marginal bottleneck. Binding-month counts expose seasonal restrictions that annual averages can hide. Kollsnes’s processing/export basis remains provisional.

Legacy definition and vintage

The 2024 run is a historical reconstruction using revised inputs. The full-year 2025 official-Snøhvit scenario is a provisional revised-input reconstruction / diagnostic. Neither is a genuine forecast made before its target year.

The retained December 2023 and June 2024 legacy outputs are forecasts at those origins, with public release timing unverified. The current workbook was modified on 8 October 2026 and has an October 2026 cached cutoff. Its historical formulas use raw/reference allocations; the sheet name “Forecasts” does not make those cells pre-period forecasts.

Historical SJ upload!L links Forecasts!M, which converts total Nybro entry to bcm. Separate Baltic Pipe!P Faxe and R Danish offtake cells are retained in the ledger. The original five legacy groups are preserved. No proxy is relabelled as independently verified physical Gassco observations.

Monthly volumes use bcm/month. Calendar-adjusted capacities come directly from saved constraint tables. Annual values sum complete monthly cells; no missing values become zero. Pipeline arc flows are not added into a national export total because the same gas traverses multiple arcs.

Named legacy Gassco totals and the sum of the five allocation groups are provided separately in legacy_totals.csv. Their original accounting remainder is retained, with no adjustment to force agreement.

January–September 2026 outcomes remain locked and unscored. No live data retrieval, Excel recalculation or optimisation occurs during report generation. Forecasts through December 2030 are not extrapolated; they require real outputs, source provenance and an authorised reporting policy.

Saved-volume, group, node-balance, disposal and capacity reconciliation checks passed. Source hashes, cell ledgers and verification evidence are included in the package.
